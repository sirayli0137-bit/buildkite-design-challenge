Slide 3 — PRD foundation

What the PRD set up

Before any pixels, the PRD pinned down four things. Every later decision pushes against one of them.

Business goal

Less time lost to broken builds

Every minute spent figuring out what failed is a minute not shipping. The build page is where that minute is won or lost.

User goals

Two different questions

Jordan — "Tell me what to do next."

Sam — "Tell me how big this is."

Two archetypes

Different jobs, same surface

Jordan — on-call dev. Reacts to a pager. Wants to act fast.

Sam — platform engineer. Watches the system. Wants the wider picture.

Technical constraints

Where it lives

Inside an existing component. Match Buildkite's styles. Must scale from 1 failure to 5+, and from 6-step pipelines to 30+.

These four set the rails. The next 12 slides ride on them.
