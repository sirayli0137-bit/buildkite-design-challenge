Slide 9 — Information architecture + layout

Where things live, and what shape they take

Before drawing anything, I split the build page into three layers, then evaluated layouts for the middle one.

Three-tier information architecture
Tier 1
Collapsed bar
Status, name, time. The "what's the headline" layer.
Tier 2 — what I built
Expanded panel
What failed. What's blocked. What to do. The summary layer.
Tier 3
Step list + logs
The full detail. Source of truth.
The rule → each tier has its own job. Tier 2 is a summary, never a copy of Tier 3. This is the rule that later kills inline log previews.
Five layouts I evaluated for Tier 2
01
Vertical stack
Tight at 1 failure. Awkward at 5.
02
Horizontal rows
Same pattern at 1 or 5. Stacks naturally.
→ V7 production
03
Split panel
Wastes space when only 1 failure.
04
Card grid
Too much chrome around small content.
05
Condensed bar
Not enough room for context.
