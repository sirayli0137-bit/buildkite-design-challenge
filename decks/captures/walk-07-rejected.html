Slide 7 — What I left out

What I deliberately did NOT include

Judgement shows up in what gets cut. Three calls I made on purpose, with the principle that broke the tie.

01

Inline log preview

An obvious-looking add: show the failing line or last 5 log lines right inside the failure card.
Why I cut it → Logs already live in the section right below. Putting them here would just repeat what's there. The expanded view is a summary, not a copy of the logs.
02

A row listing "Blocked: Bundle, Deploy"

Earlier variants (V3–V6) had a row showing blocked steps as chips. Removed in V7.
Why I cut it → The pipeline strip already shows blocked steps in their right place — with deep links per step. A separate list would just say the same thing twice. One element per job.
03

"AI" labels and Sparkles everywhere

Earlier versions had Sparkles icons next to suggestions and "AI" in every button label. I removed them.
Why I cut it → Plastering "AI" everywhere reads like marketing. The user's job is to fix the build, not to interact with AI. The AI source is named once inside the explanation panel — not on every button.
