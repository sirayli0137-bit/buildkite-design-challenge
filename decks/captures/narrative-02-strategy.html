Slide 2 — Strategy

Three audiences. One view.

Buildkite serves three kinds of users. The triage view has to work for all of them at the same time.

Enterprise teams

Many pipelines. Many builds running. Someone is always on call.

Needs scale + speed

Small + medium teams

Fewer engineers. Less time to learn the tool.

Needs clarity, no jargon

Individual developers

New to CI. No team to ask.

Needs an obvious next step

How this design competes

For enterprise → The row layout holds at 30+ steps and 5 failures with no layout break. The pattern dispatch table is fast and cheap to extend.

For everyone → Plain labels. Buttons next to the thing they act on. No "advanced mode" toggle.

One component. Three audiences.
