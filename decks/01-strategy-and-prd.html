Deck 1

Strategy & PRD

How a small component sits inside Buildkite's product story.

Where Buildkite stands

"Buildkite is the control plane for software delivery, while customers can choose where and how their build infrastructure runs."
  • Hybrid architecture is the moat: enterprise-grade control + scale + security
  • Strong with mature engineering teams

The expansion challenge

"From powerful CI/CD for enterprises → To CI/CD that grows with you, from first project to global scale."
  • Buildkite wants to expand into SMB and individual developers
  • Currently feels oriented to platform engineers / DevOps experts
  • Down-market expansion ≠ removing power. It means progressively revealing power as users mature

Why this component matters

A micro-observability surface

The fastest path from "something broke" to "I know what broke and what it blocked."

Two strategic differentiators converge here:

  • Observability — why builds fail, which steps blocked downstream
  • Developer-first experience — error explainability, accessible to product engineers

Plus the natural entry point for AI-assisted debugging (Strategy §7.3).

Three-tier architecture

TierSurfaceRead timePurpose
1Progress bar (collapsed)~2sAt-a-glance health
2Expanded header (this)~10sTriage: what broke, what's blocked
3Step list + logsMinutesDeep investigation

Tier 2 must be faster than scrolling down. A summary layer, not a duplicate of the step list below.

Six design principles

Each one came back as a deciding vote during iteration.

1. Failure-firstfailure is the headline
2. Triage, not deep-divelogs are one click away
3. Blast radius claritywhat's blocked, by name
4. Accessible to non-expertsplain language + colour + text
5. Status-awarepassed / running / failed each have their own framing
6. Compact and containeda header, not a dashboard
